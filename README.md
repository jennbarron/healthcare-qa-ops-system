## Overview

This repository documents a real-world healthcare Quality Assurance (QA) operations system designed to support analyst onboarding, standardized evaluation, KPI reporting, and operational visibility.

Rather than treating QA as scorekeeping alone, this project demonstrates how standardized processes, calibration, and analytics can improve consistency, reduce ambiguity, and support continuous improvement across operations.

All information has been intentionally sanitized. No proprietary client data, internal documentation, protected health information (PHI), or confidential workflows are included.

---

## Why This Exists

Quality Assurance is often viewed as a compliance function that measures performance after work is completed.

This project approaches QA differently.

By standardizing evaluation criteria, documenting scoring logic, improving calibration, and building operational reporting, QA becomes a decision-support system that helps leaders identify trends, reduce variation, improve coaching, and strengthen operational consistency.

The objective was not simply to score work—it was to build a repeatable framework that supported better operational decisions.

## Problem

A new cohort of approximately 50 Quality Assurance analysts required rapid onboarding, standardized evaluation criteria, and reliable performance reporting aligned with client expectations.

Existing processes relied heavily on manual tracking, inconsistent calibration, and limited operational visibility.

Without standardized documentation and reporting, maintaining consistent scoring across analysts became increasingly difficult.

## Solution Architecture

The framework followed a structured operational workflow:

1. QA onboarding and expectations alignment
2. Standardized scorecard development
3. Client calibration sessions to validate scoring consistency
4. QA execution within the operational platform
5. Automated data extraction and KPI aggregation
6. Weekly reporting and trend analysis
7. Continuous calibration and process refinement

---

## QA Scorecard & Calibration

The scorecard was designed to improve consistency while reducing evaluator variance.

Key components included:

- Standardized QA questions aligned with client policy
- Defined scoring criteria
- Calibration sessions with client stakeholders
- Documentation supporting consistent evaluation
- Ongoing refinement based on operational feedback

## KPI Framework

Example metrics included:

- QA Accuracy Rate
- Critical Error Rate
- Category-Level Compliance Trends
- Review Volume vs. Analyst Capacity
- Week-over-Week Performance Movement

The reporting framework supported both operational management and executive-level visibility.

## Reporting & Analytics
